Common questions

Commercial boiler & hot water BMS, explained.

Who manages commercial boiler and plant room BMS controls?

Boilerpulse designs, integrates and repairs boiler and plant room controls at the BMS level for commercial and industrial buildings across the UK, including sequencing, interlocks and hot water compliance. 24/7 emergency, same-day callout available.

Why isn't my commercial boiler responding to the BMS?

Usually a failed communications link between the boiler controller and the BMS head-end, a faulty sensor input, or a sequencing fault after a firmware or setpoint change. We diagnose and restore control on-site.

What is Legionella-compliant hot water monitoring?

Continuous BMS monitoring of hot water storage and outlet temperatures against ACOP L8 / HSG274 thresholds, with logged data and alarms if temperatures drift out of the compliant range.

Can you integrate a new commercial boiler into an existing BMS?

Yes. We commission new boiler plant into an existing BMS head-end without replacing it, mapping points and setting up sequencing, schedules and alarms to match the rest of the building.

How much does commercial heating BMS repair cost?

Cost depends on the fault type, the number of boilers or zones affected, and the existing BMS platform. We provide a fixed quote after a site survey.

Do you work with Trend, Tridium, Honeywell, Schneider or Johnson Controls?

Yes. We integrate boiler and plant room controls with Trend, Tridium/Niagara, Honeywell, Schneider EcoStruxure and Johnson Controls Metasys, regardless of who installed the original system.

What is boiler sequencing and why does it matter?

Sequencing controls which boilers fire and in what order across a multi-boiler plant room, based on demand. Poor sequencing wastes fuel and shortens boiler life; correct sequencing is set and tuned at the BMS.

Can you retrofit an existing plant room with modern BMS controls?

Yes. We retrofit ageing plant rooms with modern BMS controls, replacing outdated panels and wiring in sequencing, interlocks and remote monitoring without a full mechanical strip-out.
